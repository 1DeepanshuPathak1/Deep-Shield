How this verdict was reached
The two detectors never see each other's answer. The picture is judged by scoring every face across 20 sampled frames; the sound is judged by measuring 17 acoustic properties and putting them to a vote across 100 decision trees. When both agree, confidence is high. When only one flags, that usually means a partial edit — a real recording with a swapped face, or genuine footage given a cloned voice.
