How this verdict was reached
Three independent checks run, and none sees the others' answer. Face swapping scores each face across 20 sampled frames for blending artefacts. AI generation ignores faces and measures the texture, sensor noise and frequency signature of the footage itself — this is what catches a fully generated scene, which has no face to swap. Audio measures 17 acoustic properties and puts them to a vote across 100 decision trees. Agreement between checks raises confidence; a single flag usually means a partial edit, such as real footage given a cloned voice.
