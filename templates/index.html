How this verdict was reached
Two independent checks run on every clip. The face-swap check scores each face it finds for the blending artefacts left by swapping one face onto another; a clip is flagged once at least 5 faces look synthetic. The AI-generation check ignores faces entirely and instead measures the texture, sensor noise and frequency signature of the footage itself, because a fully generated scene has no face to swap — it is synthetic all the way through. Either check alone is enough to raise a flag, and the two agreeing is the strongest signal.

Frame-by-frame evidence

Measured properties of each sampled frame. Sharpness is edge contrast — generators produce cleaner edges than a lens. Noise floor is sensor grain, which generated frames largely lack. Fine detail is high-frequency energy, often over-represented in upsampled output. Frame change is how much moved since the previous sample; generated video drifts inconsistently.
