How Deep Shield works

Two independent detectors, each trained for the signals that give its medium away.

Audio pipeline

Every clip is reduced to 17 acoustic measurements — 13 MFCCs plus spectral centroid, chroma, zero-crossing rate and RMSE. A Random Forest of 100 decision trees votes on whether those measurements look natural.

Video pipeline

Twenty frames are sampled across the clip. MTCNN locates every face, and a ResNet50 + EfficientNetB0 ensemble scores each one for the blending and texture artefacts that face-swapping leaves behind.

Evidence, not verdicts

Each result shows how many faces were checked, how they split between authentic and manipulated, the confidence behind each one, and exactly which rule produced the final answer.
